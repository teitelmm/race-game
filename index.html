Slingshot Glf

Nine floating holes over open water. Grab the ball, pull back, let it fly.

Practice

Play any hole on its own. Your best score on each hole is saved.

How to play

  • Shoot Press on the ball, pull back like a slingshot, and let go. The further you pull, the harder you hit.
  • Look around Drag anywhere off the ball to turn the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom. A and D keys also turn.
  • Putt / Chip A putt rolls. A chip flies over gaps and water. Switch with C or Space.
  • Hazards Water costs a stroke and puts you back where you hit from. Sand slows the ball down.
  • Trick shots Bank it off walls, bounce it off bumpers, or dunk it straight in for bonus callouts.
